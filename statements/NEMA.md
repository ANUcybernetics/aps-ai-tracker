AI Transparency Statement

The National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA) uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) in a safe and responsible way to help with preparedness, response, recovery and risk reduction for all hazards, and to improve organisational efficiencies. In doing so, we will keep humans at the centre of our decision making and:

• prioritise human rights\
• protect personal information\
• respect First Nations’ data.

As NEMA develops, leads and coordinates the Australian Government’s emergency management activities, we will explore innovative uses of AI. In doing so, we will focus on managing the risks, strengthening organisational maturity and identifying applications that support NEMA’s objectives. We are committed to ensuring that AI enhances the effectiveness and quality of our services while maintaining the highest standards of ethics, safety and public trust. NEMA is developing its policy on the use of AI to support transparent and responsible AI use and alignment with relevant legislative and regulatory requirements, including the [Policy for the responsible use of AI in government](https://architecture.digital.gov.au/responsible-use-of-AI-in-government)**,** the [Voluntary AI Safety Standard](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/voluntary-ai-safety-standard)\*\*\*\* and the [Australian Artificial Intelligence Ethics Principles](https://architecture.digital.gov.au/australias-artificial-intelligence-ethics-principles).

### Monitoring and Accountability

NEMA’s AI use cases are led by our Chief AI Officer who is supported by the Accountable AI Official. These officials ensure the safe, ethical, and responsible use of AI technologies at NEMA.

They also make sure that AI use meets changing laws, ethical standards, and public expectations.

To support these efforts, NEMA has an AI strategy to ensure that the use of AI is ethical, transparent and secure. These elements include:

- **Governance Committee** : Delivered through our Data, AI and Technology Governance Committee and chaired by the Chief AI Officer. This committee reviews data and AI practices, including their appropriate use, access, quality, security, and introduction, to ensure their alignment with agreed data ethics. NEMA’s internal guidance establishes accountable ownership and impact assessment, with the Committee providing risk-based oversight and escalation, including for high-risk use cases requiring Digital Transformation Agency notification and ongoing reassessment.
- **Ethics Statement** : Designing and implementing a NEMA specific ethics statement for the use of AI. This sets appropriate expectations for the use of AI and aligns with the whole of government AI principles.
- **Human Oversight** : Embed human mechanisms to review AI outputs that contribute to decision making activities and that staff retain accountability.
- **Cybersecurity Standards** : Managed by NEMA’s appointed Chief Information Security Officer, and overseen by NEMA’s appointed Chief Security Officer, we adhere to the Australian Government’s Protective Security Policy Framework (PSPF). As an interim measure NEMA has formally adopted the Department of Home Affairs’ position and guidance for the ethical and secure use of AI. This includes the Home Affairs Risk Management Plan for using AI (AI RMP). As we explore our usage patterns this may require us to implement a NEMA specific AI Risk Management Plan which incorporates a range of AI risks and controls.

#### AI Patterns

As we continue exploring AI use cases, we will consider potential applications across NEMA. We maintain an internal AI Use Case Register to track our implementation in line with our AI strategy.

#### Our current usage patterns:

| Usage pattern                             | Description                                                                                                                                                                 | Examples                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Workplace productivity                    | Supports routine work, communication, collaboration and personal productivity without materially changing business processes or administrative outcomes.                    | Meeting summaries, email drafting, document summaries, presentation drafting, translation support, calendar assistance, action tracking and routine secretariat support. |
| Analytics                                 | Identifies patterns, generates insights, forecasts outcomes or makes recommendations from structured or unstructured information to support evidence-based decision making. | Risk analysis, demand forecasting, fraud detection, anomaly detection, sentiment analysis, trend identification, service performance reporting and predictive modelling. |
| Decision making and administrative action | Uses AI to support a decision or administrative action.                                                                                                                     | Eligibility decision systems for government services or making recommendations based on submitted applications.                                                          |

| Usage pattern         | Description                                                             | Examples                                                            |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |

NEMA safeguards against risks and ensures responsible AI usage by publishing ongoing AI transparency statements when changes occur to provide visibility on the usage and management of AI.
