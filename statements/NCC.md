## **National Competition Council: Artificial Intelligence Transparency Statement**

The National Competition Council acknowledges the Digital Transformation Agency’s[ Policy for the Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Governmen](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy)t, which establishes the Australian Government’s approach to the safe and ethical use of AI.

The NCC does not employ its own staff. Instead, the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) provides the NCC with the necessary corporate and secretarial support to enable it to carry out its functions effectively, under the terms of a memorandum of understanding. As the NCC relies on the ACCC for these services, the NCC’s artificial intelligence governance aligns with the ACCC’s AI Transparency Statement.

For details on the ACCC’s approach to AI use, please refer to the [ACCC’s AI Transparency Statement.](https://www.accc.gov.au/about-us/accc-role-and-structure/governance-and-accountability/artificial-intelligence-ai-transparency-statement)

**_Review of this statement_**

This statement was lasted reviewed in February 2026. It will be reviewed and updated annually by the ACCC, when a significant change is made to the agency’s approach to AI, or when the NCC becomes aware of any new factor that materially impacts the statement’s accuracy.
