# Artificial Intelligence Transparency Statement

The Commonwealth Grants Commission (CGC) is committed to the safe and responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI). The CGC recognises that AI provides opportunities to improve productivity, enhance analytical capability and support staff in delivering high-quality advice. This must be done while preserving accountability, transparency and public trust.

The CGC follows the [ _Policy for the responsible use of AI in government_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy) , which provides principles and mandatory requirements for agencies relating to the accelerated and sustainable adoption of AI. Consistent with the Policy, this AI Transparency Statement outlines the CGC’s approach to AI adoption.

### **Our approach to AI**

The CGC encourages the responsible exploration and adoption of AI consistent with the [ _Policy for the responsible use of AI in government_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy). The CCG is looking to build its AI capability, foster innovation and continuous improvement, and ensure staff are equipped to use AI confidently and responsibly.

### **How we use AI**

The CGC's endorsed AI applications (ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot 365 and GovAI) are used internally to support workplace productivity, with the aim over time to leverage AI to enhance our analytical capabilities, improve our advice and increase operational efficiency.

The CGC employs AI in the domain (using the Digital Transformation Agency’s [ _Classification system for AI use_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/resources/use-classification) of corporate and enabling, in the following ways:

- analytics for insights, where AI is used to assist staff with coding

- research and analysis

- workplace productivity, where AI is used to assist staff with communication, document summarisation and document creation.

The CGC does not use any AI systems that directly interact with the public or make decisions that affect individuals without human oversight.

### **Our governance arrangements**

The CGC has an AI Accountable Official who is responsible for overseeing the implementation of the [ _Policy for the responsible use of AI in government_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy). The CGC has a Chief AI Officer who, working with the AI Accountable Official, is responsible for championing and driving AI adoption in the CGC.

The CGC has an AI team to promote the safe and effective use of AI across the agency. The team identifies emerging AI capabilities, explores potential uses and use cases relevant to the CGC’s work, and encourages staff to identify opportunities where AI may assist with administrative, analytical and corporate functions. The AI team conducts an annual staff survey to monitor the use and effectiveness of AI. The team also holds regular training sessions on how to use AI appropriately.

The CGC has an AI policy. The policy includes the CGC’s strategic position on AI adoption, guidance for staff on the responsible use of AI in the agency as well as what to do in the event of an incident. The Australian Public Service Commission’s AI in Government Fundamentals course is available for all staff and, as outlined in the CGC’s policy, its completion is mandatory before staff can begin using any AI tools.

### **Compliance**

The CGC’s commitment to digital innovation aligns with the Australian Government’s [ _Data and Digital Government Strategy_](https://www.dataanddigital.gov.au/strategy/missions/government-for-the-future#adopt_emerging_technologies) , the [ _National AI Plan_](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/national-ai-plan) and the [ _APS AI Plan 2025_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/australian-public-service-ai-plan-2025/what-we-plan-achieve).

The CGC complies with all applicable legislation and regulations, including the [ _Protective Security Policy Framework_](https://www.protectivesecurity.gov.au/) (PSPF) and [ _Information Security Manual_](https://www.cyber.gov.au/business-government/asds-cyber-security-frameworks/ism) (ISM).

The CGC have robust processes, regular reviews and audits, and proactive risk management strategies, including staff training programs, to support the responsible and effective use of AI.

Working with Treasury as our Information and Communications Technology provider, the use of AI is governed by our IT Acceptable Use Policy, Treasury’s Information Security Policy, and the [ _Policy for responsible use of AI in government_](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy).

All CGC AI policies and procedures, including this transparency statement, are reviewed and updated annually or when significant changes occur. The most recent review of this transparency statement was in July 2026.
